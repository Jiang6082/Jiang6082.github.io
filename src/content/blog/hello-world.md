Welcome to my new site! I finally got around to putting together a place to
write things down, share projects, and post photos.

## Why a personal site?

Social platforms come and go, but a site I own is mine. I wanted somewhere to:

- write longer-form notes without a character limit,
- link out to the things I'm building, and
- keep a small gallery of photos I'm proud of.

## What's next

I'll be posting about projects I'm working on and things I'm learning. To add a
new post, drop a Markdown file in `src/content/blog/` — that's it.

You can even embed images:

```md
![A caption](https://res.cloudinary.com/your-cloud-name/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto/some_photo)
```

Thanks for stopping by.
